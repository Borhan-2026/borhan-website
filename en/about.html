About us

Borhan Trading was founded in Austria to make high-quality natural products from Iran accessible to the European market. What began with natural salts and decorative stones is growing into a broad range around nature and health. Our principle: everything we carry is 100 % natural, free of chemicals — and serves your wellbeing.

Our strength is the direct connection to the regions of origin: we know the producers personally, inspect goods on site and organize the entire journey to delivery in Europe — including documentation and customs.

Our standard is simple: reliable quality, traceable origin, and partnerships that last beyond a single shipment.
